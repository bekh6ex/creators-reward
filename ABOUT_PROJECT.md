# About the project

## Background

I've seen many people in my life. Although there are certainly egoistic people 
and people who try to make others miserable, the vast majority of the people
I've met in my life are good and honest, try to help and support each other and are ready
to share with those in need. And many (if not the majority) of the problems and
bad behaviors come from misalignment and misunderstanding.

## The Problem

The modern world is diverse and complex. It's not enough to read one media to get
objective information, as well as there are multiple different tools and 
projects we have to rely on to live through our day. Many of them are free, and 
I would like to support them so they can continue working and improving.
Let's say I've started donating regularly 10 EUR per month (e.g. Patreon 
subscription) to one of my favorite media websites. On average, I read 5-10 
different medias. I added a donation to another one and another one. And then
my monthly bill became 50 EUR. Still not all of them. I will add more, adjust 
and correct the sums for the previous ones, so my final monthly bill is 50 EUR.
Now I've opened Wikipedia and decided to support them. Another 10 EUR per month.
Then, some time passed, I've discovered more interesting creators and media outlets
whom I want to support, but adding a subscription for each one means I need to
review all the others because I cannot afford to pay more. The world changes month 
after month, and my content consumption is changing with it. I discover new
creators and gradually stop reading/watching the others. My kids are not babies
anymore, I'm not interested in the baby topics now. I occasionally use GIMP 
and Inkscape to
edit some photos or draw some pictures, I like the software, I want it to be developed further, but
the value it provides to me is too small to go and set up another subscription
that I have to track and adjust.

This becomes a constant burden
of managing my monthly donation budget, which I don't have time to do, and 
in the end, when there is an opportunity to support someone else, I do not take it. 
I do not add another Patreon subscription because it means that I have to 
review all my existing subscriptions and adjust. Clicking the button now and figuring
out later already backfired multiple times for me, so I just sadly look at the 
support link and close the window.

I've talked with multiple people and found that they share the same proble with
me when it comes to supporting projects or creators: the main source of 
hesitation is not that they are not ready to pay. Most of the time, creators 
ask for a tiny amount of money (e.g. Wikipedia's yearly campaign "If everyone 
reading this gave $3, our fundraiser would be done within an hour"). 
The problem is that they have to manage their budget and track all the donations,
each time deciding if they still want to support each project and how much.


## Positive example

Due to my profession, I work a lot with different cloud providers and have an account
in many of the popular ones. I've never had a problem with the monthly costs,  
and I've never hesitated to register for a new one, and the reason is quite simple.

When you use them, you pay only for the services you use: start a big performant
server – $2-3 per day.
At any time, I can stop the server or delete the files and not pay anymore.
There are also a lot of services that they offer, where you pay for the 
usage only: store several files – 0.01 EUR per month, 
cloud database – 0.01 EUR 10K requests, ...
On top of that, most of them allow you to configure a monthly limit, and if you 
exceed it, they will automatically notify you when they predict that you are
over your monthly budget and stop the server if you reach it

Sometimes I do forget to stop the server, I get a notification, sigh and stop it.
For some things that I've set up, I don't care, because I pay per usage and I
do not use them anymore.


## Vision

What if we had a platform that would allow us to support creators and projects
in the same way cloud providers charge for their services? Taking care of the
budget, not charging me for the creators I don't read/watch anymore, adjusting 
the donations to my needs and how much I actually consume what different creators
produce.
I would like to see a platform that would allow me to support creators and projects
using a pay-as-you-go model. All the money I spend on the platform would be
used to support the creators and projects I support, I don't need to manage my
budget. I can review the donations when I want adn adjust the spendings, but 
not because I have to.


## Goals

1. Create a platform that allows me to support creators and projects using a pay-as-you-go model.
2. Make the platform as simple as possible to use. Any person should be able to use it,
   regardless of their technical skills.
3. Collect and store as little user data as possible. We do not want to track our users.
   Use encryption, obfuscation and data anonymization techniques to protect the data that we have to store.
4. Make the platform as transparent as possible.

## Non-goals
1. Not another cryptocurrency-based platform. 
   Cryptocurrencies are certainly interesting considering privacy aspects, 
   but:
   1. They require a lot of technical skills
      to use, and the vast majority of people are not ready to use them. Our platform should be
      accessible to everyone and not just the tech-savvy ones
   2. There are quite some legal issues with cryptocurrencies in most of the countries
